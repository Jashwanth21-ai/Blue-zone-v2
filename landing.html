A file open to everyone

A SMALL SETTING. A BIG DIFFERENCE.

Who can read your secrets?

A service configuration should only be accessible to the account that needs it. Learn to read Linux permissions and spot unnecessary exposure.

rw-OWNER
r--GROUP
r--EVERYONE

READ-ONLY TRAINING SNAPSHOT

analyst@bluezone ~ $ ls -l

-rw-r--r--  service  service.env
-rw-------  analyst  notes.txt
-rwxr-xr-x  root     healthcheck.sh

# Every local user can read service.env.

Look at the final three permission characters. An r means everyone can read this file.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little clarity.
Then, let’s get to work.

Do I need cybersecurity experience?+

No. The first path starts with networking, Linux permissions, and web security concepts. Later missions build on those foundations.

What do the practice labs run?+

The V1 labs use fictional evidence and a contained, read-only command simulation. You can inspect logs and configurations without installing a virtual machine or touching live systems.

How does the tutor work?+

Built-in lesson explanations and hints are available immediately. If the administrator configures an AI provider, you can opt in to AI guidance for a question.

What do I earn as I progress?+

Passing assessments earns XP, unlocks missions, and adds to your saved learning record. Complete paths to earn achievements, then finish the capstone to earn Blue Horizon. These are training achievements, not accredited certifications.
